How SkillBridge Works

Complete transparency into our data sources, skill extraction, normalization, and scoring math.

Transparency & Math
📡

1. Job-Market Data Sourcing

SkillBridge aggregates public developer postings across public feeds (Remotive, Arbeitnow, Jobicy) and verified benchmark datasets. Skill frequencies are computed from active job descriptions to reflect real-world employer requirements.

🧠

2. Skill Extraction & Context Parsing

When you upload or paste a resume, our parser extracts technology tokens and analyzes context windows for proficiency keywords without hallucinating fake scores.

🔄

3. Skill Normalization

We normalize synonymous technologies (e.g., React.js = ReactJS = React, Node = NodeJS, PostgreSQL = Postgres = SQL) so your experience maps accurately to industry job descriptions.

📐

4. Deterministic Role Matching Formula

Every role has core weighted skills (weights 1 to 10). The base fit score is calculated as:
Match = (Sum of [Candidate Level % × Skill Weight]) / Total Role Weight
